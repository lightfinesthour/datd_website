Demon At The Door is a beautiful 2D adult adventure in active development.

Set in a mansion off in the woods with a small town nearby. A young man returns from six years of private school to get his life in order, and the mysterious circumstances surrounding the death of his parents open onto a much greater mystery.

Find out what happened to his family and uncover the secrets of the house he grew up in.

Along the way you will meet a cast of unique and sexy characters, explore beautiful locations, play mini-games, and much more.

Support on Patreon opens soon, with exclusive rewards: voting on content releases, new characters and upcoming scenes, sketches, unreleased art, and more.

The Firedreams VN Engine

The data-driven visual novel system that powers Demon At The Door. Built on Godot 4.6 and designed so writers and artists can build with it directly.

Godot 4.6 JSON-defined scenes FFmpeg video Encrypted release pipeline Cross-platform export

Author everything in JSON

Scenes, dialogue, branches, and entire cutscenes are defined in plain JSON. Writers and designers iterate freely, and version control treats every change like a normal text diff.

Cinematic scene directives

Precise positioning, transitions, slide and fade animations, camera shake, lightning flashes, and picture-in-picture overlays. All expressed declaratively, frame-perfect, and easy to tweak.

Real video, including transparency

Integrated FFmpeg video playback supports MP4 and WebM out of the box, with alpha-channel video for ghostly overlays, animated character bursts, and full-screen cinematics.

Production-ready release pipeline

Ship with confidence: AES-256 asset encryption, SHA-256 filename obfuscation, and a one-command export pipeline keep your art and writing protected from casual extraction.

Cross-platform from day one

One project file, one set of content, and exports to Windows, Linux, and Steam Deck via Godot's mature export pipeline. Mobile and macOS targets follow the same path.

Built for the long game

Quest systems, time and energy, multi-room exploration, save state: everything we needed to build a real visual novel is being lifted out of the game and made reusable.
