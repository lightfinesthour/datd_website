PRIVACY POLICY FOR THE DEMON AT THE DOOR DISCORD BOT
====================================================

Service:     Demon at the Door Discord bot
Operator:    Firedreams Studio
Contact:     dream@firedreams.studio
Effective:   31 August 2026
Last update: 28 October 2026


SUMMARY
-------
The bot stores what is needed to do three jobs: manage members of the
Demon at the Door Discord server, grant Discord roles and rewards to
people who support the project on Patreon, and keep a record of what is
posted in the server. That last part means messages you send in the
server, and direct messages you send to the bot, are logged and kept.
Your data stays with Firedreams Studio and the services listed in
section 6, and is used only for the purposes listed in section 4.


DETAILS
-------

1. Who this covers
   This policy applies to the Discord bot operated by Firedreams Studio in
   the Demon at the Door Discord server. It is separate from Discord's own
   privacy policy and Patreon's privacy policy, which also apply to you
   when you use those services.

2. What the bot stores

   Discord data
   - Your Discord user ID
   - Your username and server nickname
   - The IDs of the servers and roles you have in servers where the bot
     is installed
   - Timestamps of bot actions involving you (for example when a role was
     granted or removed)
   - Moderation records, if a moderator uses the bot to issue a warning,
     timeout, or ban: the reason, the moderator, and the date

   Message records
   - The content of messages you post in channels of servers where the
     bot is installed, together with the author, the channel, the time
     sent, and any edits or deletions
   - Attachments and links you post, or references to them
   - Direct messages you send to the bot itself, including their content,
     attachments, and the time sent
   This log is kept for recordkeeping: to settle disputes, to review
   moderation decisions, and to keep a history of the community. Treat
   anything you post in the server, or send to the bot in a direct
   message, as a permanent record that the server's operators can read
   back at any time.

   Patreon data (only if you choose to link your Patreon account)
   - Your Patreon user ID
   - Your membership status and pledge tier
   - The email address associated with your Patreon account, where Patreon
     provides it, used to match your pledge to your Discord account and to
     send you the patron emails described in section 4
   - The link between your Patreon account and your Discord account
   Patreon members, on a paid tier or the free tier, also get a patron
   page at patrons.demonatthedoor.com; paying patrons also get patron
   emails. What that service stores, for free members too, is described
   in the Firedreams Studio privacy policy: https://firedreams.studio/documents/datd_patreon_app_privacy-policy.txt

3. The limits of what the bot reaches
   The bot collects the data listed in section 2 and nothing further. Its
   reach covers channels of servers where it is installed and direct
   messages addressed to it. Direct messages between you and other users,
   voice channel audio, and messages in servers without the bot stay
   outside that reach. Payments run entirely through Patreon, so the
   information Firedreams Studio receives about your pledge is limited to
   your Patreon user ID, membership status, tier, and email address.

4. Why the data is used
   - To grant, update, and remove Discord roles that match your current
     Patreon tier
   - To deliver rewards and perks tied to those tiers, including direct
     messages from the bot when you join, change tier or leave
   - To email paying patrons about their membership (welcome, tier
     change, end of membership) and, unless switched off on the patron
     page, about new game builds. Free members get no emails or direct
     messages from the bot.
   - To run normal server administration: welcome messages, role
     self-assignment, moderation, and anti-spam
   - To keep a record of server activity: reviewing reports and disputes,
     checking moderation decisions after the fact, recovering deleted or
     lost content, and keeping a history of the community
   - To answer support questions such as "why did my supporter role
     disappear?"

   These purposes are the full list. Your data stays out of advertising,
   profiling, and any sale or rental to third parties.

5. Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK users)
   Where the GDPR applies, the bot processes your data on the basis of
   your consent (you invite the bot's actions by joining the server and by
   linking your Patreon account) and legitimate interests (running a safe,
   functioning community server, keeping a record of it, and honouring the
   rewards supporters have paid for).

6. Who the data is shared with
   - Discord Inc., because the bot operates on Discord's platform
   - The hosting provider that runs the bot and its database, which stores
     the data on our behalf and is bound to act only on our instructions
   - Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which sends the patron emails on our
     behalf and receives your email address and the message content
   Server moderators and administrators can read the message log, the
   moderation records, and the supporter roles. Beyond these, your data is
   disclosed only where the law requires it.

7. How long the data is kept
   - Everything the bot holds about a server is deleted within 30 days of
     the bot being removed from that server

8. Your rights and choices
   You can ask, at any time, to:
   - have it deleted
   
   Leaving the server also stops all further processing.

9. Security
   Data is stored on access-controlled servers, and access is limited to
   Firedreams Studio and the people who operate the bot.

10. Children
    The bot is for people who meet Discord's minimum age (13, or higher
    where local law requires it). Data belonging to a child below that
    age is deleted as soon as we learn of it.

11. Changes
    If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same
    address with an updated date. Significant changes will be announced in
    the Discord server.

12. Contact
    Questions? Email dream@firedreams.studio.
